# Mild dares: quick, silly, nobody gets hurt. One per line.
Tell a joke. If nobody laughs, tell another one.
Do your best impression of another player until someone guesses who it is.
Talk like a pirate until your next turn.
Speak only in questions until your next turn.
Give every player at the table a sincere compliment.
Sing the chorus of the last song you listened to.
Show the last photo in your camera roll.
Do 10 squats while everyone counts out loud.
Balance a spoon on your nose for 10 seconds.
Say the alphabet backwards in under 30 seconds.
Give a 30 second dramatic speech about your favourite vegetable.
Do your best catwalk across the room.
Hold a plank for 30 seconds.
Invent a handshake with the player on your left and perform it.
Tell the table your most embarrassing school memory.
Do your best robot dance for 15 seconds.
Name 10 countries in 10 seconds.
Keep a straight face while the table tries to make you laugh for 20 seconds.
Describe your day so far as a movie trailer voice-over.
Stand on one leg until your next turn.
Read the last text you sent out loud, in a newsreader voice.
Do an infomercial for the nearest object.
Try to lick your elbow. Commit to it.
Hum a song until someone guesses it.
Let the table pick an accent. Use it until your next turn.
Mime your morning routine in 20 seconds.
Rap about the player on your right for four lines.
Do your best evil laugh. The table rates it out of 10.
Tell the table who here would survive longest in a zombie apocalypse, and why.
Spell your full name backwards out loud.
Do a slow-motion replay of your greatest sporting moment.
Pretend to be a tour guide showing the table around this room.
Whisper everything you say until your next turn.
Thumb-wrestle the player across from you.
Make up a short poem about tonight.
Balance on one leg with your eyes closed for 15 seconds.
