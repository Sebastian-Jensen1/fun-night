# Wild dares: bolder, more embarrassing, worth more sips. Still safe and still optional. One per line.
Let the player on your left post a story on your phone. You may veto once.
Let the group choose your profile picture for the next 24 hours.
Swap a piece of clothing with the player on your right until the game ends.
Text a friend outside the room using only emojis and show the reply when it comes.
Let the table go through your camera roll for 20 seconds.
Call someone and sing them happy birthday, whether it is their birthday or not.
Do a dramatic reading of your most recent search history.
Let another player write a status on your phone and leave it up for an hour.
Serenade the player across from you with a full verse.
Do a 30 second stand-up routine about the player on your left.
Let the table style your hair and keep it that way until the game ends.
Hand your phone to the player on your right: they send one message to anyone you choose.
Dance with no music for 30 seconds. Everyone else films.
Do your best impression of every player at the table, one by one.
Show the table the most embarrassing photo on your phone.
Let the table pick a word. Every time you say it tonight, you drink.
Speak in rhymes until your next turn. Every miss is a sip.
Give a wedding speech for two other players at the table.
Do 15 push-ups, or as many as you can while the table counts.
Let the player on your left draw something on your arm with a pen.
Narrate everything the player on your right does until your next turn, nature-documentary style.
Reveal your screen time for today and defend it.
Re-enact a famous movie scene. The table has to guess the film.
Sit on the floor until your next turn.
Let the group give you a new name. Answer only to that name for the rest of this game.
Call a friend and try to sell them the nearest object.
Do an interpretive dance of how your week has been.
Trade seats with whoever the table votes for, and keep their drink company.
Let the table pick your next drink mixer.
Keep a spoon in your mouth and say a tongue twister three times.
Wear your shoes on your hands until your next turn.
Tell the table your worst date story.
